# Brag Plan: SubhChandra Education

## What is this app?
SubhChandra Education is Bihar's premier full-stack higher education portal and counselling platform offering AI-powered college matching, authentic degree admissions, and direct Bihar Student Credit Card (MNSSBY) aid.

## The angle
Higher education admissions are full of confusion, misinformation, and high broker fees. SubhChandra Education brings clarity, honesty, and AI-powered intelligence to students across Bihar and India—connecting 15,000+ students to 45+ premier NAAC-accredited universities with guaranteed financial aid.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Fast typographic entrance on deep emerald glassmorphism:
"Choosing a college shouldn't feel like a gamble."
Snap reveal: The verified SubhChandra Education emblem with Bihar Govt award badge.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1: AI College Finder in Action (3-8s)**: Sleek search and stage selector (10th, 12th, UG, PG) with instant college recommendations.
- **Moment 2: Top Tier Universities & Placements (8-13s)**: Real student placement cards (Marwadi, DIT, Graphic Era, packages up to ₹42 LPA) with verified recruiter logos.
- **Moment 3: Bihar Student Credit Card Assistance (13-17s)**: 100% financial guidance and zero-hassle loan sanction support under MNSSBY.

## Outro / punchline (17-20s)
"Right course. Right career. Zero guesswork."
SubhChandra Education logo, Patna Head Office, helpline +91 88008 85175, and URL `subhchandra.com`.

## User flow worth showing
Student enters stream & stage → AI College Finder filters 45+ universities → instant admission guidance & student credit card assistance unlocked.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
